    *   Correlation between D and E: 0.994
    *   Correlation between E and F: 0.992

    *   **Column I**: Mean is 64.09, significantly higher than its median of 44.23, with a maximum value of 403.94 and a standard deviation of 58.51. Skewness is 1.646.
