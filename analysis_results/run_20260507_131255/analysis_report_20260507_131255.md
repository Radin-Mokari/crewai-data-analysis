# Executive Summary

This report details the initial data preparation and statistical analysis for the California Housing Prices dataset. The dataset, comprising 20640 rows and 10 columns, was cleaned by addressing 207 missing values in the 'total_bedrooms' column, resulting in a `df_clean` of 20433 rows. No duplicate rows were identified. A key finding from the preliminary analysis is the strong positive correlation between `total_rooms` and `total_bedrooms` (0.93038), as well as between `total_bedrooms` and `households` (0.979728), indicating collinearity among these features. Conversely, `longitude` and `latitude` exhibit a strong negative correlation (-0.924616).

# Data Overview

The analysis was performed on the `California_Housing_Prices.csv` dataset, which is not a time-series dataset. The initial raw dataset, `df_raw`, contained 20640 rows and 10 columns.

The columns in `df_raw` include:
- longitude
- latitude
- housing_median_age
- total_rooms
- total_bedrooms
- population
- households
- median_income
- median_house_value
- ocean_proximity

The dataset consists of 9 numeric columns and 1 categorical column, identified as:
- **NUMERIC_COLUMNS**: longitude, latitude, housing_median_age, total_rooms, total_bedrooms, population, households, median_income, median_house_value
- **CATEGORICAL_COLUMNS**: ocean_proximity

# Quality & Cleaning

The data preparation phase involved checking for duplicates and handling missing values.

No duplicate rows were found in the `df_raw` dataset.

Missing values were identified in the `total_bedrooms` column.

## Missing Values
| Column         | Count of Missing Values |

These 207 rows with missing `total_bedrooms` values were dropped from the dataset.
The `df_raw` dataset, initially with a shape of (20640, 10), was transformed into `df_clean` with a final shape of (20433, 10) after dropping the rows with missing values. All column data types were inferred to be suitable for analysis, with numeric columns as float64 and 'ocean_proximity' as string type.

# Recommendations

Based on the initial data analysis, the following recommendations are made:
- **Address Collinearity**: The strong positive correlations among `total_rooms`, `total_bedrooms`, `population`, and `households` suggest potential multicollinearity. Further investigation into these relationships, possibly through feature engineering (e.g., creating ratios like rooms per household or bedrooms per room) or dimensionality reduction, could be beneficial for model performance.
- **Categorical Feature Analysis**: The `ocean_proximity` categorical column, while not included in the correlation matrix, should be explored through techniques like one-hot encoding for its potential impact on `median_house_value`.
- **Target Variable Exploration**: Further analysis of `median_house_value` distribution and its relationships with all other features, including `median_income` (which is often a strong predictor in housing datasets), is crucial for understanding factors influencing housing prices.
